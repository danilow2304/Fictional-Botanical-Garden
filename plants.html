Sunflower

Sunflowers are large, bright flowers that follow the sun throughout the day. They are native to North America and are popular for their vibrant colors and seeds.

Height 5-8 Feet.
Bloom Time Midsummer- Early Fall
Sunlight Full Sun
Water Low to Moderate

Rose

Roses are beautiful flowering plants that come in many varieties. They are native to Asia and are beloved for their fragrance and colorful blooms.

Height 1-20 Feet.
Bloom Time Late Spring-Early Fall
Sunlight Full Sun
Water Low to Moderate
